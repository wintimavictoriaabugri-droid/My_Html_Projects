Welcome to Our Indian Cuisines Selection

Explore a variety of authentic dishes from different regions of India.

North Indian Dishes

  • Dal Makhni: A classic dish made with black lentils and kidney beans, simmered in a creamy sauce.
  • Paneer Tikka Masala: Marinated paneer cheese served in a spiced gravy.
  • Chole Bhature: A combination of spicy chickpeas (chole) and fried bread (bhature).

South Indian Dishes

  • Idli and Dosa: Fermented rice cakes and crepes, often served with Sambar and chutney.
  • Biryani: A fragrant rice dish cooked with spices and meat or vegetables.
  • Fish Curry: A popular coastal dish with fish in a tangy and spicy sauce.

Desserts

  1. Gulab Jamun: Soft, deep-fried berry-sized balls soaked in a sugary syrup.
  2. Rasgulla: Spongy, sweet cottage cheese balls in light syrup.
  3. Kulfi: Traditional Indian ice cream in various flavors like mango or pistachio.
